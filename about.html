Welcome to The Pele Guesthouse: A home away from home!

Every great place has a story, and ours is built on love, legacy, and family.
The Pele Guesthouse is named after my late father, Mr. Motale Mabote, who was affectionately known by his soccer nickname, "Pele." Although he passed away when I was still a toddler, his spirit, warmth, and the joy he brought to those around him have always been a guiding light in my life. Opening this guesthouse in Carletonville is my way of honoring his memory and keeping his name alive — not just as a tribute to the man he was, but by creating a space where people feel genuinely welcomed and cared for, just as he would have wanted.

Comfortable, Unpretentious, and Welcoming

Tucked away in a quiet, peaceful neighbourhood in Carletonville, The Pele Guesthouse isn't trying to be a flashy, luxury hotel.Instead, we focus on what truly matters when you are away from home: comfort, peace of mind, and genuine hospitality.
Whether you are visiting Carletonville for work, school, passing through on a trip, or visiting family, we want you to kick off your shoes, take a deep breath, and feel right at home.
